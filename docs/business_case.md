# Business Case

## Background

A marketing team runs advertising campaigns across Facebook Ads and Google Ads.

Campaign data is collected daily but stored in multiple database tables, making cross-platform performance analysis difficult.

Management needs a centralized dashboard to monitor campaign effectiveness and support marketing decisions.

## Business Objectives

The dashboard should help answer:

- Which campaigns perform best?
- Which platform generates higher ROI?
- Does increasing advertising spend result in more leads?
- Which campaigns should receive additional budget?
- Which campaigns should be optimized or paused?

## Business Value

The dashboard provides:

- centralized reporting
- campaign comparison
- marketing funnel analysis
- budget efficiency analysis
- interactive campaign exploration
